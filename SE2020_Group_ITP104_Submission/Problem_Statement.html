Problem Statement: CALIDI Boutique Mobile Solution

1. Introduction

In the rapidly evolving retail landscape, boutique fashion houses face increasing pressure to provide a seamless, mobile-first shopping experience. CALIDI Boutique initially relied on fragmented systems that struggled to keep pace with modern consumer expectations and complex back-end logistics.

2. The Problem

  • Inventory Invisibility: Manual stock tracking led to frequent out-of-stock disappointments and inaccurate inventory levels.
  • Fragmented Order Fulfilment: Lack of an integrated pipeline caused delays in processing orders and coordinating with riders.
  • Static Engagement: Lack of automated loyalty systems made it difficult to retain high-value customers.

3. The Proposed Solution

The CALIDI Boutique Mobile Application was developed as a comprehensive MERN-stack solution to centralize all retail operations, featuring centralized product hubs, digital checkout, rider management, and a tiered loyalty system.

4. Objectives

Transform CALIDI into a data-driven digital platform that increases operational efficiency, eliminates inventory discrepancies, and improves customer lifetime value (CLV) through personalized rewards.
